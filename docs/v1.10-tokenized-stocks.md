# V1.10: tokenized stocks and wider universes

Status: implemented locally on branch `worldsfair` (not committed).
Migrations `0026`, `0027`. Sources researched 2026-10-04.

V1.10 widens three universes and adds tokenized stocks from their issuers'
own registries. The model does not change: `tokenized_security`, `TRACKS`,
`TOKENIZES`, deployments and chains exist since V1.4–V1.6.

```text
Apple Inc. common stock (equity, ISIN US0378331005 as the issuers state it)
   ▲ TRACKS                 ▲ TOKENIZES                 ▲ TRACKS
Apple xStock            Apple Inc. • Backpack        Apple • Robinhood Token
(Backed, ISIN CH…)      tokenized security           (RHJ)
   ▲ REPRESENTS            ▲ REPRESENTS                 ▲ REPRESENTS
solana:…/token:Xs…      solana:…/token:AAPL…         eip155:4663/erc20:0x…
   └─ Jupiter price (by mint)  └─ Jupiter price (by mint)
```

Each token is its own instrument. None is the share, none inherits the
share's issuer or identifiers, and no two issuers' tokens are merged.

## 3. Nasdaq-100 through QQQ

Like SPY for the S&P 500, the QQQ ETF's holdings are a practical proxy,
not the official constituent file. Rows of type `COM`, `ADR` and `DRNY`
with a valid CUSIP and USD currency are shares; cash, futures and
collateral rows are excluded. Members are keyed by CUSIP exactly like SPY
members (`spy:<CUSIP>`; the prefix is historical), so the 86 securities in
both ETFs are one instrument each. The others are imported the same way
(SEC ticker table for the issuer's CIK and the primary listing's MIC).

## 4. Matching a token to its share

The share is found **by identifier only**:

- A US ISIN's NSIN is its CUSIP (ISO 6166: the US numbering agency is CUSIP
  Global Services). An issuer's `US<CUSIP><check>` therefore names the
  imported equity keyed by that CUSIP. Non-US ISINs are not decomposed; their
  products are excluded.
- Backpack states the CUSIP itself.

The registry's ticker must then **agree** with the equity's listing symbol
(one class-share rule, `BRK-B` = `BRK.B`). This is a check, never a way to
find a match. It matters: on 2026-10-04 Backpack's API stated wrong CUSIPs
for some names (`JPM.US` 16161A108, `MS.US`, `WFC.US`). Those matched no
imported equity and are reported. A wrong CUSIP that names another imported
equity is rejected by the ticker check. The check also caught Robinhood's
`SATS` token, whose ISIN names the equity listed as `ECHO`; it is excluded
for review.

The same matches give an imported equity the ISIN its tokens' issuers state
(`isin:US0378331005` now resolves to Apple). This is a stated identifier,
never one constructed from a CUSIP. V1 objects keep their pinned identifiers.

## 5. Issuers and relationships

| Issuer | Legal form (issuer documentation) | Edge | `ISSUED_BY` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backed Assets (JE) Limited, LEI `984500001AB7C6C7F577` | "tokenized tracker certificates that track the value of publicly traded equities" (assets.backed.fi) | `TRACKS` | the issuer |
| Backpack Securities | "Claim on SPV holding the underlying assets", "redeemable 1:1 for the real underlying security" (support.backpack.exchange, Tokenized Securities) | `TOKENIZES` | none: the SPV is not named |
| Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited, LEI `984500ADFHQZ9D6B9A29` | tracker certificates (V1.6, Final Terms) | `TRACKS` | the issuer |
| BTech Holdings Limited (ADGM), LEI `25490057XTWK16I5YJ22` | certificates backed 1:1 by shares held with a custodian (Binance's launch announcement, 2026-06-11) | **none** (see below) | the issuer |

LEIs are from GLEIF (`api.gleif.org`), matched to the legal names in the
issuers' own documents.

Notes:

- **Robinhood:** the registry's `isin` field is undocumented. V1.6 verified
  it equals the underlying's ISIN for NVIDIA and Apple. V1.10 uses it for
  every product, so these `TRACKS` edges rest on that field together with
  the ticker check. The NVIDIA product keeps its V1.6 ingestion from reviewed
  Final Terms; its registry row is skipped by the build.
- **bStocks:** Binance's list states only the share's ticker, never an ISIN
  or CUSIP, so no edge to the share is asserted (decision, 2026-10-04:
  token only). A bStock is its own product of BTech Holdings, deployed on BNB
  Smart Chain, with its Binance spot book (`AAPLBUSDT`, USDT, `mid`,
  confirmed in `exchangeInfo`). The list is Binance's web backend, not its
  documented API. Linking needs each product's ISIN from its ADGM
  prospectus, reviewed like V1.6's Final Terms.
- **xStocks:** older `b`-prefixed Backed products (`bNVDA`) are separate
  products with their own ISINs, and track the same shares.
- Product names come from the registries (`Apple xStock`,
  `Apple • Robinhood Token`, `Apple Inc. - Backpack Sec.`).
  A bStock is named by its share's ticker (`AAPL bStock`). Symbol aliases
  are `AAPLx` and `AAPL.US`. A Robinhood token's own symbol is the share's
  ticker (`AAPL`), so it is **not** an alias; query it by name, ISIN or
  CAIP-19.

## 6. Deployments and prices

- Deployments are kept only on chains Undrly stores: Solana (Backed, Backpack),
  Robinhood Chain (RHJ) and BNB Smart Chain (bStocks; chain binding added in
  `data/reference/onchain.json`). The others (Ethereum, BSC, Arbitrum and more for
  xStocks) are not modelled yet.
- `universe build` writes `data/universe/deployments.json`. `undrly-collect
  onchain` ingests it after the chains: each deployment `REPRESENTS` its
  product, asserted by the registry record that `seed` stored.
- **Jupiter** prices each Solana deployment by its mint: `usdPrice`, Jupiter's
  price of the token from onchain trading. It is stored as `basis:
  aggregated`, `priceType: last`, with no venue and no bid/ask.
  - Mints without enough liquidity have no `usdPrice` and are reported
    missing.
  - `stockData.price` (the share's price as the issuer reports it) is not
    the token's market and is not read.
  - For scaled-UI tokens, `usdPricePrescaled` is not read either. Undrly
    stores `usdPrice` as stated.
- Polling: 40 mints per request, one request a second, a sweep a minute.
  The request URL is the record key, so migration `0026` raises
  `source_records.record_key` to 2,048 characters. That keeps the replay
  key's index entries under PostgreSQL's row limit. Kraken and Alpaca keep
  packing to 256.

## 9. Operational notes

- A new database is required. Seeding on a database where `onchain` already
  created Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited (V1.6) fails on its LEI: the
  snapshot now declares that issuer with a pinned id. On a new database,
  `seed` creates it and V1.6's Final Terms ingestion finds it by LEI.
- Order: `universe fetch` → `universe build` → `seed` → `onchain` → `run`.
  `universe fetch` makes about 25 CoinGecko requests, 7 s apart, and waits a
  minute on HTTP 429. Expect 5–10 minutes.
- The Coinbase sweep grows with the top 250 and outlasts the 30 s
  `mean-venue-mid-v1` window for more pairs, so those pairs aggregate
  Kraken alone in a pass (docs/v1.1-universe.md §5, known limitation).

## 11. Wider universes (crypto top 500, S&P 1500)

| Universe | Source | Priced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `crypto-top500` | CoinGecko `coins/markets`, pages 1 and 2 (250 each) | Kraken/Coinbase USD (as before) and **Binance spot USDT** |
| `sp400`, `sp600` | SSGA MDY and SPSM holdings (same workbook as SPY) | Alpaca IEX |

- **Crypto.** A Binance market is found exactly like Kraken's and
  Coinbase's: CoinGecko's own Binance tickers name the coin, and Binance's
  `exchangeInfo` confirms the trading market (base, quote). A USDT market
  is priced in Tether, its own market, and is never averaged with USD
  markets.
  - Page 1 and page 2 are two requests, so a coin that moved between them
    is one member.
  - `crypto-top100` and `crypto-top250` are page 1's first ranks.
    `crypto-top500` names page 2 as its record, the last page read.
- **Equities.** MDY and SPSM members are keyed by CUSIP like SPY's, so a
  security held by several ETFs is one instrument. The import steps are the
  same: SEC ticker table for the issuer and MIC, Alpaca IEX feeds.
- **Perpetuals.** Binance USDⓈ-M perpetuals were imported here first and
  then removed (decision, 2026-10-05): perpetuals stay Hyperliquid's alone.
- **Binance spot polling.** Book-ticker requests are packed up to 2,000
  characters (about 120 symbols), within the record key's 2,048.
- **Fetch time.** `universe fetch` now also pages CoinGecko's Binance
  tickers and looks up GeckoTerminal pools (§13). Expect 20–30 minutes
  with the public APIs' 429 pauses.

## 12. One row per asset, in US dollars

Decision, 2026-10-04: the market list shows every asset once, in USD.

- **Conversion.** A coin or bStock priced only on Binance in USDT gets a
  derived USD market: `X/USD = X/USDT × USDT/USD`
  (`convert-via-stablecoin-v1`, migration `0028`). The second leg is
  Tether's own USD market (Kraken and Coinbase). It is computed by the
  collector whenever either leg changes, like a V1.9 cross, and keeps both
  legs' observations in `canonical_quote_legs`. The USDT market itself is
  unchanged and is never averaged with USD markets.
- **Declarations.** `universe build` declares a conversion for every
  member whose only market is a Binance USDT book: 126 on 2026-10-04 (39
  coins, 87 bStocks). Perpetuals keep their settlement unit (USDT, USDC).
- **List.** `/v1/markets` leaves out a market in any unit other than USD
  when the same asset has a USD market (BTC/USDT, USDT/IDR). They stay
  queryable; FX rates keep their own rows.
- **Result (first pass).** 124 of 126 conversions priced; the crypto list
  is 308 rows for 308 coins.

## 13. Charts for Solana tokens (GeckoTerminal)

Decision, 2026-10-05: tokens priced by Jupiter get bars, so their markets
have a 24-hour change and a chart. Jupiter has no OHLC API, so bars come
from the token's deepest onchain pool, as GeckoTerminal reports it.

- **Pools** (`universe fetch`, or `universe pools` alone). For each Solana
  token Jupiter prices at fetch time (the price only selects tokens; it is
  not stored), `tokens/{mint}/pools` lists its pools with DEX, sides and USD
  reserve. `tokens/multi` was tried first and rejected: it names one top
  pool, by volume, and often none.
- **Declarations** (`universe build`). The deepest pool by USD reserve with
  at least 1,000 USD becomes a `geckoterminal` feed on that DEX's venue
  (`Raydium (CLMM) (Solana)`), symbol `b:<pool>` or `q:<pool>` for the
  token's side, unit USD, `TRADES_ON` the DEX. Shallower or missing pools
  are reported, without bars.
- **Bars** (`history bars`). Hourly (30 days) and daily (a year) OHLC in
  USD for the token's side, one request each per pool with a quote, 10
  requests a minute (the free tier answers HTTP 429 above that, though it
  documents 30) and a minute's wait on HTTP 429. Prices are JSON
  numbers, kept as written (scientific notation expanded exactly). The
  pool's USD volume is not kept.
- **Quotes do not change.** The token's price stays Jupiter's aggregate;
  only `market_bars` gain the pool. Its 24-hour change is therefore the
  pool's, which can differ from Jupiter's price by the pool's spread.
- **bStocks** need no new source: `history bars` already backfills every
  Binance market, and a bStock's USD chart is `X/USDT × USDT/USD` from
  those bars (§12).

## 14. What the market list shows for a token (2026-10-05)

- Only token markets with a quote are listed (decision, 2026-10-05: a
  token Jupiter does not price is left out; it was briefly listed as "No
  price"). One without bars shows "No data" for the 24-hour change and the
  chart.
- A pool or a bStock book has no bar in an hour without trades. The 24-hour
  change then runs from the last close at or before 24 hours before the
  latest bar: an hour without trades is an hour without a price change.
- Result (new database, after `history bars`): 197 of the first 200 token
  rows priced, 133 with a 24-hour change, 135 with a chart.

## 15. Aggregate freshness (2026-10-05)

With 500 coins a Kraken sweep takes about 40–55 s and a Coinbase sweep
about 90 s, longer than the 30 s `mean-venue-mid-v1` window. Between sweeps
dozens of aggregates aged past 30 s and the market list showed them without
a price, a different set on every refresh. The API now serves an aggregate
for 120 s after its `asOf` (one sweep); the method's own 30 s input window
in the collector is unchanged.

## 16. Bars for Coinbase-only coins (2026-10-05)

`history bars` had no Coinbase source, so the 20 coins whose USD market is
Coinbase's alone had no 24-hour change or chart. It now backfills Coinbase
Exchange candles (`/products/{id}/candles`, newest 300 hours and 300 days,
one request each) for every Coinbase market without a Kraken feed for the
same pair. `history bars --source <id>` backfills one source only.

## 17. Bars kept current by `run` (2026-10-05)

Bars used to arrive only through `history bars`, so 24-hour changes and
charts aged from the last backfill. `undrly-collect run` (not `--once`) now
refreshes them itself, on a second database connection:

- one minute after start, then every hour: the last 2 days of hourly bars
  of every bar source (Kraken, Hyperliquid, Alpaca, Binance and the other
  stablecoin venues, Coinbase-only markets, GeckoTerminal pools), and
  Alpaca's daily bars (equities' session statistics are daily);
- every 24th run, the first included: 3 days of daily bars of every source;
- 2 s added after each request, so the quote collector keeps its rate
  limits. An hourly run takes about 45 minutes; one longer than an hour
  starts the next at once;
- failures are logged (`bars refresh: …` in the collector log) and retried
  the next hour. `history bars` remains the backfill of 30 days and a year.

## 18. Six more currencies against USD (2026-10-05)

For a payments product pricing in 22 currencies, the six without a USD
rate were added (excluded since V1.2 for want of a machine-readable source):

| Pair | Source | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USD/CNY | `fed-h10` (`RXI_N.B.CH`) | as published, CNY per USD, daily noon rate in New York, released weekly |
| USD/SAR, USD/BND | `cbm` | `MMK per USD ÷ MMK per X`, both per one unit |
| USD/VND, USD/KHR, USD/LAK | `cbm` | the same, X quoted per 100 units |

- **Why the CBM table.** The Central Bank of Myanmar publishes every
  reference rate against MMK in one JSON table. It sets USD/MMK and
  derives the others from Thomson Reuters cross rates (its own rate page),
  so the ratio of two rates of one table is that cross rate, free of the
  kyat rate. Checked on 2026-10-02: USD/SAR 3.7545 (peg 3.75), USD/CNY
  6.705 (Fed H.10: 6.711).
- **Units.** The API states none; CBM's own table does (KHR, LAK, VND, JPY
  and IDR per 100). The normalizer accepts only the reviewed units
  (`CBM_UNITS`); any other currency is an error, as before.
- **Precision.** The ratio is rounded to the fewer significant digits of
  its two rates: USD/VND 26000 and USD/LAK 22500 carry three (CBM states
  8.08 and 9.35 per 100), about ±0.06%. These are reference rates for
  pricing, published once a business day, not executable quotes.
- Rejected: Bank Indonesia's transaction rates (no KHR; VND and LAK at two
  decimals, about ±1%), and the IMF's representative rates (refuse
  non-browser clients).
