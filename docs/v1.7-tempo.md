# V1.7: Tempo payment and settlement identity

Status: research complete (2026-09-28); production ingestion **GO for one
asset** (pathUSD on Tempo Mainnet), issuer **unresolved**; implemented locally
on branch `worldsfair` (not committed).

The question: how does an onchain payment asset relate to fiat currency,
other stablecoins, its issuer, its deployment and FX, without treating
money-like objects as identical? For pathUSD the truthful answer is small:

```text
USD (currency, ISO 4217) ◀──TRACKS── pathUSD (crypto_asset) ◀──REPRESENTS── deployment eip155:4217/erc20:0x20c0…0000
                                         (no ISSUED_BY: legal issuer unresolved)            │ DEPLOYED_ON
                                                                                            ▼
                                                                                 Tempo Mainnet (chain eip155:4217)
EUR ─ EUR/USD ─ USD   (existing FX graph, separate; reached through the USD node)
```

## 4. Payment architecture

- **TIP-20**: Tempo's native token standard for stablecoins and payment
  tokens, "a superset of ERC-20" (so CAIP-19 `erc20` applies), with
  `currency()`, `quoteToken()`, transfer memos (32 bytes), supply caps,
  pause, role-based access, TIP-403 transfer policies.
- **Stablecoin DEX** (enshrined, `0xdec0…`): each USD TIP-20 pairs against a
  quote token; quote tokens form a tree rooted at pathUSD.
- **Payment lanes**: ~94 % of blockspace reserved for TIP-20 payments.
- **Transfer memos**, virtual deposit addresses, fee sponsorship
  (`feePayer`), Zones (private execution; testnet).

## 5. Fee / gas model

"Tempo has no native token." Fees are denominated in **attodollars**
(10⁻¹⁸ USD) per gas and paid in any **USD-currency TIP-20** with enough
Fee AMM liquidity against the validator's preferred token; the Fee AMM
converts between them. pathUSD is the fallback fee token. A wallet may show a
placeholder "native balance"; there is none.

Undrly does not model gas; the facts that matter for identity are that fee
tokens must declare `currency == "USD"` and that pathUSD is a protocol
predeploy.

## 8. Issuer semantics

Tempo's docs say "pathUSD is issued by Bridge". Bridge's own sources do not
confirm it: the Bridge Stablecoin Terms apply to "all stablecoins ('Bridge
Stablecoins') issued by Bridge Building Inc ('BBI')", redeemable "for one U.S.
dollar with BBI", but name no stablecoin and never pathUSD; Bridge's API docs
(including its Tempo guide) never mention pathUSD; Bridge operates several
legal entities (Bridge Building Inc, S.A., Limited). The redemption statements
also differ (Tempo: "redeemed back to USDC through Bridge"; BBI terms: "for
one U.S. dollar").

**Decision:** no `ISSUED_BY` edge. The issuer's legal entity cannot be
established from an authoritative issuer source, and no LEI is published.
Tempo (the chain) is never recorded as the issuer.

## 9. Fiat / reference-currency semantics

TIP-20 specification: "The `currency` field identifies the reference asset
that 1 unit of the token is designed to be worth — **not** what the token is
called or what it is **denominated** in." It is set at creation and "cannot be
changed afterward".

| Question | pathUSD | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Denominated in USD? | Tempo calls it "USD-denominated", but the protocol field is explicitly not a denomination | TIP-20 spec |
| Designed to be worth 1 USD (tracks USD)? | **yes, as a protocol declaration** (`currency() = "USD"`) | chain + spec |
| Redeemable for USD? | unverified (Tempo: to USDC via Bridge; BBI terms: USD, but not shown to cover pathUSD) | §8 |
| Parity guaranteed? | no (BBI terms for Bridge stablecoins disclaim third-party parity; pathUSD itself unconfirmed) | — |
| Settles obligations in USD? | no: transfers move pathUSD; fees are *denominated* in attodollars but *paid* in tokens | fee spec |

## 10. Settlement semantics

Payments on Tempo settle in the TIP-20 token transferred; cross-stablecoin
payments are DEX swaps; fees are paid in a USD TIP-20 converted by the Fee
AMM. None of this makes a token equal to USD or to another token. Undrly
records no payment route and no conversion.

## 11. Proposed Undrly mapping

| Object | Undrly |
| --- | --- |
| Tempo Mainnet | `chain` `eip155:4217` (from `eth_chainId`) |
| pathUSD | new `instrument`, class `crypto_asset`, name `pathUSD`, symbol alias `pathUSD` (the token's own onchain symbol) |
| its deployment | `deployment` `eip155:4217/erc20:0x20c0000000000000000000000000000000000000` |
| USD | existing `currency` (ISO 4217 `USD`) |

Edges: deployment `REPRESENTS` pathUSD; pathUSD `TRACKS` USD; `DEPLOYED_ON`
projected. Nothing to USD Coin, Tether, or any other stablecoin.

## 12. Relationship requirements

| Candidate | Verdict for pathUSD → USD |
| --- | --- |
| `DENOMINATED_IN` | **no**: the spec says `currency` is not the denomination |
| `SETTLES_IN` | **no**: transfers settle in pathUSD itself |
| `REPRESENTS` | **no**: deployment → instrument only; pathUSD is not USD |
| `DERIVES_FROM` | **no**: not a derivative |
| new `PEGGED_TO` / `REDEEMABLE_FOR` / `BACKED_BY` | not needed / not supported: redemption and backing are unverified issuer claims |
| **`TRACKS`** | **yes**: "the reference asset whose price the token is designed to track" is exactly V1.6's `TRACKS` meaning (value designed to follow the object, no claim on it, no identity) |

Change: `TRACKS` gains endpoint (instrument → **currency**) (migration 0022).
Existing (instrument → instrument) use by RHJ's tracker is unchanged.

## 13. Provenance strategy

```text
POST rpc.tempo.xyz eth_chainId                         ─▶ raw (tempo-rpc) ─▶ chain eip155:4217 (only if 4217)
POST rpc.tempo.xyz [eth_chainId, name, symbol,           ─▶ raw (tempo-rpc, one batch record)
                    currency, decimals @ 0x20c0…0000]      ─▶ checks: chain 4217; name/symbol/currency/decimals equal the
                                                              reviewed binding (fail closed)
                                                           ─▶ pathUSD (found by its deployment, else minted), alias pathUSD,
                                                              deployment, REPRESENTS, TRACKS USD — all naming that record
```

The binding (`data/reference/onchain.json`, `tip20Assets`) states the
**protocol predeploy address** (a genesis constant in Tempo's predeploy table,
not a registry entry that can change) and the expected onchain metadata.
The chain itself is asked; any mismatch (another chain, another name, symbol,
currency or decimals) rejects the record before anything is written.

## 14. Unknowns and ambiguities

- pathUSD's legal issuer (which Bridge entity, if any) and its redemption
  asset (USD or USDC) are unresolved (§8).
- Backing "1:1 by high-quality USD-denominated reserves" is a claim in chain
  documentation, not in an issuer attestation Undrly can read.
- USDC.e's bridge semantics (Stargate/LayerZero) are not modelled.
- The tokenlist is PR-curated; not an identity authority.
- No market data: pathUSD's DEX prices are onchain orderbook state, not
  collected.

## 15. Go / no-go

**GO** for Tempo Mainnet identity and pathUSD (asset, deployment, `TRACKS`
USD), each proven by the chain itself against protocol documentation.
**NO** issuer edge. **NO** USDC.e. **NO** settlement endpoint (§16).

## 16. Settlement-context endpoint: not added

The question "what financial context surrounds this payment asset?" is
answered by existing primitives:

| Wanted | Already served by |
| --- | --- |
| canonical asset, class | `/v1/resolve`, `/v1/explain` |
| deployment, chain, CAIP-19 | `/v1/explain?q=caip19:…`, graph `deployments` |
| reference currency | `TRACKS` edge (explain / graph) |
| issuer | `ISSUED_BY` (none for pathUSD) |
| FX context | `/v1/quote/EUR/USD` (live and reference), `/v1/history` |
| provenance, timestamps | explain provenance, quote `asOf` |

A new endpoint would only join these, and the join is exactly the place where
a reader could mistake context for an executable conversion. Decision: no
endpoint; document the traversal instead.

## 17. Tempo primitives for later

Useful but out of scope: TIP-20 `quoteToken` tree (a DEX market structure),
Stablecoin DEX orderbook prices (a venue market per pair), Fee AMM pools,
transfer memos (transaction data, not identity), TIP-403 policies, Zones.
