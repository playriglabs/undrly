# V1.9 — Live FX through stablecoin venues

Status: local, branch `worldsfair`. Not committed.

## 1. Purpose

Most V1.2 FX pairs are central-bank **reference** rates: published once a
business day, with no bid/ask, no hourly bars and so no 24-hour statistics.
Mayarin (a payments product) will read Undrly as its live rate source and
needs current rates between its assets: 22 fiat currencies (IDR SGD MYR THB
PHP VND BND MMK KHR LAK USD JPY CNY HKD EUR GBP AUD CAD AED SAR BRL MXN),
five stablecoins (USDC USDT EURC PYUSD USDG), ETH and BTC.

V1.9 adds:

1. **Stablecoin/fiat markets** from venue order books (USDT/IDR, USDC/THB,
   USDT/PHP, …): six new venues plus new Kraken and Coinbase books.
2. **Derived FX** `USD/X = (USDT/X) / (USDT/USD)` for eight currencies that
   had no live source, with the new method `cross-via-stablecoin-v1`.
3. A clear status for every Mayarin currency: live, derived, reference
   only, or unsupported. No guessed numbers.

## 3. Model

- A stablecoin/fiat market is a **crypto-asset market in a fiat unit**,
  exactly like BTC/USD: subject = the stablecoin instrument (V1's `usdt`,
  `usdc`; a new `EURC` instrument), unit = the currency. No new instrument
  class. Two or more books → `mean-venue-mid-v1` (unchanged, 30 s window);
  one → `latest-observation-v1`.
- New currency nodes: HKD, AED, BRL, MXN, CNY, SAR. New FX instruments:
  USD/PHP (moved from `excluded`), USD/HKD, USD/AED, USD/BRL, USD/MXN, the
  last four in a new universe **`fx-global`**.

### `cross-via-stablecoin-v1`

Declared per pair in `quote_derivations` (migration 0024) with both legs:

```text
USD/IDR = USDT/IDR ÷ USDT/USD
          numerator   denominator   (the same stablecoin)
```

1. Both legs' **canonical quotes** must exist and be venue-based (basis
   `venue`, or `aggregated` from venues). A reference rate or another cross
   never enters a cross.
2. `price = numerator / denominator`, exact decimal division rounded half
   to even to the **fewer significant digits** of the two prices: a ratio
   cannot be more precise than its inputs (the rule V1.2 uses for inverted
   rates). `undrly_core::quote::cross_rate`; mirrored in TypeScript as
   `v1.crossRate`.
3. With both legs' books, `bid = num.bid / den.ask` and
   `ask = num.ask / den.bid` (the conservative side of each).
4. `basis: derived`, `priceType: mid`, `asOf` = the older leg. Inputs are
   the legs' observations, kept in `canonical_quote_legs` with the pair each
   priced (`canonical_quote_inputs` only admits the pair's own observations).
5. The collector recomputes a cross whenever either leg's canonical quote is
   refreshed, and removes it when a leg has none. A pair declared as a cross
   is never priced by its own observations: USD/IDR keeps its JISDOR feed,
   stored and available, but the canonical quote is the cross.

It assumes USDT trades at one price against every fiat. A de-peg against
one currency moves that cross: hence `basis: derived`, and the legs stay
inspectable at `/v1/quotes` of each leg.

## 4. API

- `/v1/quote` of a cross: `DerivedQuoteV1` (`basis: derived`, method
  `cross-via-stablecoin-v1`), `receivedAt` the latest leg receipt, fresh
  while `asOf` is within the default 300 s window (continuous clock).
- `/v1/market` statistics of a cross: window **`rolling_24h_closes`**, from
  the cross of the legs' hourly closes at the 25 consecutive hours ending
  with the latest. `open` is the close 24 hours before; `high`/`low` are the
  max/min **of those closes**, not an intrabar range; `volume` is `null`.
- `/v1/markets` sparkline of a cross: its last 24 hourly cross closes.
- `/v1/history/{cross}?interval=1h|1d` (default `1d`): the cross's closes
  (`priceType: mid`, `basis: derived`). `?series=reference` returns the
  pair's own reference feed (USD/IDR's JISDOR).
- `/v1/candles/{cross}`: `404 no_data`, pointing at `/v1/history`. A cross
  has no venue bars, and its high/low cannot be derived from the legs'.

### Published series (reference rates, averages)

A reference rate or average is not traded, so its `marketStatus` stays
`null`, but `/v1/market` now states how it changed, from the stored values
of the feed behind its canonical quote:

- **`rolling_24h`** from the source itself: with `GOLD_API_KEY` (gold-api's
  free key: 10 OHLC requests an hour; its terms permit commercial use and
  say nothing on redistribution), the collector polls `/ohlc/{XAU,XAG,XPT,XPD}`
  over the 24 hours before each poll, hourly. The window is stored as stated
  (`reference_windows`, migration 0025, with its raw record) and served while
  under 2 hours old: `open` is gold-api's price 24 hours before, `high`/`low`
  its range, `volume` `null`.
- **`rolling_24h_observations`**: a series polled within the day (gold-api
  metals, every 60 s): the last value at or before 24 hours ago against the
  latest, max/min in between. Until 24 hours are stored the statistics are
  `null`: the previous value would be a minute old, not a publication.
- **`previous_publication`**: a daily (EIA, central banks) or monthly (World
  Bank) series: the latest value against the one before, `previousClose`
  set, `from`/`to` their dates.
- The `/v1/markets` sparkline is the last value of each hour (intraday
  series) or the last 24 publications.

The dashboard tags these changes `1d` / `1w` / `1m` (by the gap between the
two values) so a monthly change is never read as 24 hours.

## 6. Data-use limitations

Every new source is registered `redistribution = unknown`, like V1.2's.
Using these rates in production (Mayarin settlement) needs each venue's API
terms reviewed first, and Binance's public API is not reachable from every
region. Until then the data is for local/private use only.
