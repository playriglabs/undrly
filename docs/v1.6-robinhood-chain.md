# V1.6: Robinhood Chain and tokenized securities

Status: research complete (2026-09-28); production ingestion **GO for one
asset** (the NVIDIA Stock Token), implemented and verified locally on branch
`worldsfair` (not committed). Every fact below is from Robinhood's own documentation, its
issuer's legal documents, its public API, or the chain itself.

The question V1.6 answers: how does Undrly represent

```text
NVIDIA Corporation (entity, LEI 549300S4KLFTLO7GSQ80)
   ▲ ISSUED_BY
NVIDIA common stock (equity, ISIN US67066G1040) ──LISTED_ON──▶ Nasdaq (XNAS)
   ▲ TRACKS                                      (economic exposure only)
NVIDIA Stock Token (tokenized_security, ISIN JE00BX9C6J83) ──ISSUED_BY──▶ Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited (LEI 984500ADFHQZ9D6B9A29)
   ▲ REPRESENTS                                   DENOMINATED_IN / SETTLES_IN ──▶ USD
deployment eip155:4663/erc20:0xd0601ce157db5bdc3162bbac2a2c8af5320d9eec
   │ DEPLOYED_ON
Robinhood Chain (chain eip155:4663)
```

without collapsing any two of them.

## 2. Network status

Robinhood Chain is "a permissionless, Ethereum-compatible Layer-2 blockchain"
built on Arbitrum (Orbit/"Dedicated Blockchains"), ETH as gas, first-come
first-served sequencing. **Mainnet and testnet are live** and publicly
queryable: public RPC endpoints are published ("rate-limited and not
recommended for production use"); production access is via providers
(Alchemy recommended).

## 3. Chain identity

| | Mainnet | Testnet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official chain ID | 4663 | 46630 |
| `eth_chainId` (chain itself) | `0x1237` = 4663 | `0xb626` = 46630 |
| CAIP-2 | **`eip155:4663`** | `eip155:46630` (not ingested) |

CAIP-2 is unambiguous: the `eip155` namespace reference is the EIP-155 chain
ID. Undrly asks the public mainnet RPC for `eth_chainId`, stores the answer
raw, and records the chain only if it is 4663; testnet can never pass as
mainnet.

## 4. Token registry and discovery

- `GET https://api.robinhood.com/rhj/assets`: the issuer's read-only asset
  registry (documented schema: `id` onchain uid, `tokenSymbol`, `tokenName`,
  `deployments[]` of `{contractAddress (EIP-55), chainId}`, multipliers,
  `status`). Rate limit 60 req/s, cached. On 2026-09-28: **195 assets, all
  `ASSET_STATUS_ACTIVE`, each with exactly one deployment, all on chain
  4663**.
- The live response has **undocumented fields**: `tokenDecimals` (18),
  `networkName`, `itnEnabled`, `atomicEnabled`, and **`isin`**. For every
  asset checked, `isin` equals the Final Terms' **ISIN of the Underlying**
  (NVIDIA: `US67066G1040`; Apple: `US0378331005`), not the product's own
  ISIN (`JE00…`). 179 are `US…`, 16 other countries; no duplicates.
- The docs' Token Contracts page says a same-named token at another address
  is not a Robinhood Stock Token: the **address on its chain** is the token's
  identity, never the symbol or name.
- Each product's **Final Terms** (legal document, per Series) names the
  product ISIN, the smart contract "serving as Securities Ledger", the
  Supported Blockchain System, and the Underlying with its ISIN and its
  issuer's LEI. This is the authoritative link between token and security.

## 5. Legal and economic structure

From the NVIDIA Final Terms (Summary §3 and §1) and the RHJ site:

- "Stock Tokens are **tokenised debt securities** issued by Robinhood Assets
  (Jersey) Limited." "The Products are **collateralised tracker
  certificates**, issued in the form of ledger-based securities
  (Registerwertrechte) … under Swiss law. The Products are undated,
  non-interest bearing, secured, limited recourse obligations of the Issuer
  that **track the market value of the Underlying**, subject to the deduction
  of certain fees."
- "The Product provides **economic exposure** to underlying securities but
  **does not grant investors any legal or beneficial rights in, or against the
  issuer of, those underlying securities**."
- Redemption: "the Issuer's obligation to pay the Redemption Amount **in
  USD** … calculated on a 1:1 basis … based on the market price of the
  relevant Underlying". "Investors are not entitled to receive physical
  delivery of the relevant Underlying."
- Collateral: "The Collateral primarily comprises the Underlying", held in a
  segregated account (custodian and broker: Alpaca Securities LLC, LEI
  9845006BZ9782C600323); a Security Agent (Security Agent Services AG) acts on
  an Event of Default. The FAQ: "backed 1:1 by the corresponding underlying
  equity".
- Product currency: USD; denomination per Product = market price of one unit
  of the Underlying.

## 6. Relationship to the traditional security

The product is **not** the share, **not** a claim on the share, and **not** a
derivative contract in Undrly's `DERIVES_FROM` sense (a perpetual or future
priced off an underlying). It is a debt obligation of another issuer whose
value, by its terms, tracks the share. Hence:

| Candidate | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `TOKENIZES` (token-form claim on the object held in custody) | **No.** Holders have no legal or beneficial rights in the Underlying and no delivery; their claim is against RHJ, secured by collateral. |
| `REPRESENTS` | **No.** Deployment → instrument only; the product is not the share. |
| `DERIVES_FROM` | **No.** Reserved for derivative contracts; the Final Terms define a tracker debt security. |
| **`TRACKS`** | **Yes.** Its own legal text: obligations "that track the market value of the Underlying". |

`TRACKS` already existed in the vocabulary (for funds, not storable). V1.6
makes `instrument TRACKS instrument` storable (migration 0021), direction
`tracker → the instrument whose market value it tracks`. Collateralization
(backed 1:1 by the Underlying) is a separate fact with no rule yet; it is
recorded here, not in the graph.

## 7. Identifier semantics

| Identifier | Belongs to | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `JE00BX9C6J83` | the NVIDIA Stock Token (Series 1) | Final Terms ("ISIN of the Product") |
| `US67066G1040` | NVIDIA common stock (Underlying) | Final Terms ("Security Codes of the Underlying"); also the registry's `isin` |
| `549300S4KLFTLO7GSQ80` | NVIDIA Corporation | Final Terms (issuer of the Underlying) |
| `984500ADFHQZ9D6B9A29` | Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited | RHJ site and Final Terms |
| Bloomberg ticker `NVDA` | the Underlying, as a ticker | Final Terms; not an identity |
| `0xd0601CE157Db5bdC3162BbaC2a2C8aF5320D9EEC` on `eip155:4663` | the token's deployment | Final Terms (Securities Ledger) and registry |
| registry `id` `0x…915f4774…` | RHJ's onchain uid for the asset | registry; not stored |

No FIGI or CUSIP is assigned to the product in any source. The underlying's
ISIN is **never** copied to the product (and the database would quarantine
such a claim: one ISIN, one node at a time).

## 8. Corporate-right semantics

- No shareholder rights: "not entitled to exercise voting rights, attend or
  participate in meetings, or exercise pre-emption rights".
- No direct dividend right; the benefit is delivered per the Terms: in
  practice dividends are **reinvested via an onchain multiplier**
  (`uiMultiplier()`, ERC-8056 "Scaled UI Amount"), which also absorbs splits
  (registry `currentMultiplier`, e.g. NVIDIA `1.000775159164630595` shares
  per token on 2026-09-28).
- Corporate actions pause trading; the issuer publishes adjustments.
- Redemption with the issuer (fees after 90 days), in USD cash.

## 9. Restrictions

Not offered to US persons (Regulation S), nor in Canada, the UK, Switzerland,
or listed sanctioned jurisdictions. Primary issuance only through Authorised
Participants (Bitstamp Global Ltd); minting window Monday 02:00 to Saturday
02:00 CET/CEST; secondary trading onchain 24/7 where available. Undrly
records identity only; none of this changes identity, and it is documented,
not modelled.

## 11. Undrly mapping

| Object | Undrly | Found / created by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Robinhood Chain | `chain`, CAIP-2 `eip155:4663` | `eth_chainId` of the public RPC, raw |
| RHJ | `entity` (company) with LEI | LEI (primary identifier) |
| NVIDIA Stock Token | `instrument`, class `tokenized_security`, ISIN `JE00BX9C6J83` | ISIN (primary identifier) |
| its deployment | `deployment` `eip155:4663/erc20:0xd060…9eec` | (chain, address), from the registry |
| NVIDIA common stock | existing V1 `equity` | ISIN `US67066G1040` |
| NVIDIA Corporation | existing V1 `entity` | untouched |

Edges: token `ISSUED_BY` RHJ; token `TRACKS` NVIDIA common stock; token
`DENOMINATED_IN` USD and `SETTLES_IN` USD (Product currency, USD redemption);
deployment `REPRESENTS` token; `DEPLOYED_ON` projected. NVIDIA common stock
keeps `ISSUED_BY` NVIDIA; the token never inherits it.

## 13. Provenance strategy

```text
POST rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com eth_chainId ─▶ raw (robinhood-chain-rpc) ─▶ chain eip155:4663
GET  …/rhj_final_terms…nvidia.pdf               ─▶ raw (rhj-final-terms, the PDF bytes)
       verified against the reviewed binding's SHA-256 ─▶ RHJ (LEI), token (ISIN), ISSUED_BY,
                                                          TRACKS NVIDIA (by ISIN), DENOMINATED_IN / SETTLES_IN USD
GET  api.robinhood.com/rhj/assets               ─▶ raw (rhj-api) ─▶ deployment (address of the asset whose
                                                    underlying isin is the binding's, on 4663) ─▶ REPRESENTS token
```

The Final Terms are a 164-page PDF; Undrly does not parse PDFs. The facts
taken from it are a **reviewed transcription** in the tracked binding
`data/reference/tokenized-securities.json`, pinned to the document's SHA-256.
Ingestion stores the fetched PDF raw first and **refuses** the facts if its
bytes differ from the reviewed hash (a new version needs review, never a
silent update). The facts then name the PDF's raw record as their source. The
binding states no contract address: the address comes only from the
registry, and the registry asset is selected by its underlying ISIN (unique in
the registry) on chain 4663.

## 14. Unknowns and ambiguities

- The registry's `isin` field is **undocumented**; its meaning (underlying's
  ISIN) is inferred from agreement with the Final Terms for NVIDIA and Apple.
  Ingestion uses it only to select the registry row, and only together with
  the Final Terms' underlying ISIN.
- The Final Terms' contract address and the registry's agree today; there is
  no machine check between them (PDF not parsed).
- No authoritative ISIN for Undrly's other equities, so no bulk import.
- Collateral (backed 1:1) has no relationship rule.
- Market data: the REST `/rhj/prices/{symbol}` returns the **underlying
  equity's** bid/ask (not multiplier-adjusted), and onchain Chainlink feeds
  are multiplier-adjusted; the token's own secondary-market price is on DEXes
  and CEXes. None is the token's price in one clear sense; no prices in V1.6.
- Redistribution terms of the API and documents are unreviewed (local demo
  only, like every source).

## 15. Go / no-go

**GO for NVIDIA only.** Chain identity, issuer identity, product identity,
legal relationship, underlying identity (by ISIN already in Undrly) and the
deployment are each established by an official source. **NO-GO for bulk**
until Undrly's equities carry authoritative ISINs.

Resolver and API (no code change was needed):

| Query | Resolves to |
| --- | --- |
| `NVDA`, `AAPL` | the equities, exactly as before |
| `isin:US67066G1040` | NVIDIA common stock (identifiers unchanged; never `JE00…`) |
| `isin:JE00BX9C6J83` | the NVIDIA Stock Token (`tokenized_security`): `ISSUED_BY` RHJ, `TRACKS` NVIDIA common stock, `DENOMINATED_IN`/`SETTLES_IN` USD, all asserted by `rhj-final-terms` |

| the bare address | `not_found` |

Graph: the token's graph lists its deployment and edges; NVIDIA common stock's
graph shows the token only as an incoming `TRACKS` edge, and no deployment.

Refresh and change detection: `onchain` refetches the Final Terms and
refuses them if the bytes no longer match the reviewed SHA-256 (review the
new document, update the transcription and hash); it refetches the registry
and reports (`REVIEW:`) any other deployment still representing the token on
the chain. Nothing is retracted automatically.

## 17. Market data (future, not integrated)

| Surface | What it is | Why not in V1.6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /rhj/prices/{symbol}` | "the raw underlying-equity bid/ask passed through as-is — not multiplier-adjusted", USD, with `generatedAt` | a price of the **underlying**, delivered by the token issuer; not the token's market |
| Chainlink feed per token (onchain) | multiplier-adjusted | an oracle reference price; needs an oracle-feed node (`PRICED_BY`) |
| DEX/CEX secondary markets | the token's own trading | venue-specific; no venue model for onchain pools yet |

A truthful token quote would be in USD per token (= per `currentMultiplier`
shares), with the multiplier explicit, and never presented as the share's
price. Corporate actions change the multiplier, not the token's identity.
Redistribution terms are unreviewed.
